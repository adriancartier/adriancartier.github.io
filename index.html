Executive Biography

A track record of bridging rigorous mathematical theory, enterprise scale, and commercial execution.

Adrian Cartier, PhD, is Head of AI Accelerator at The Hartford, leading enterprise AI and generative AI acceleration for the Fortune 100 insurer. He serves as a Member of the Board of Directors at Freight Science, an AI-driven decision-automation company he co-founded, and as Strategic Advisor & Investor for Healthcare Shares Venture Studio.

At the heart of Adrian’s work is a core passion: connecting humans with AI and technology. He believes the future is not an "either/or" choice between human intelligence and automation, but rather a symbiotic relationship where humans are supercharged by AI when leveraged properly.

Over a career spanning more than 20 years, Adrian has delivered $500M+ in operational and commercial value, led global organizations of 40+ data scientists and engineers across 13 countries, and built cloud-native decision platforms operating at massive enterprise scale.

Passionate about inspiring the next generation of leaders, Adrian frequently delivers talks to university students and student founders on AI and Startups—most recently giving a featured presentation at NC State University on building high-impact AI ventures. He also regularly guest lectures at Washington University in St. Louis.

He holds 1 issued U.S. patent (*U.S. Patent 2021009200*) with 4 additional U.S. patents pending, and has delivered keynotes to over 5,000 attendees on applied AI, operations research, and digital transformation. Adrian serves on the Alumni Board of the University of Mississippi College of Liberal Arts and is the Founder and Principal of Cartier Holdings, LLC. He holds a PhD in Mathematics from the University of Mississippi.

Adrian Cartier, PhD, is an AI and data science executive with over two decades of experience building enterprise decision platforms, commercializing software products, and governing AI systems in regulated environments. He currently serves as Head of AI Accelerator at The Hartford, leading enterprise-wide acceleration of AI and generative AI capabilities for the Fortune 100 insurer.

Adrian’s overarching vision centers on connecting humans with AI and technology. Rather than viewing artificial intelligence as a replacement for human judgment, he champions a symbiotic relationship where AI supercharges human capacity, empowering leaders and operators to make better, faster decisions at scale.

Adrian is the co-founder and former Chief Data Officer of Freight Science, an AI-powered decision-automation company serving the U.S. trucking industry, where he led the design, production engineering, and deployment of optimization and machine learning models that generated over $500 million in measured operational value. He now serves on the company's Board of Directors.

In addition, he serves as Strategic Advisor & Investor at Healthcare Shares Venture Studio, providing technical due diligence, technology roadmap counsel, and architecture evaluation for early-to-growth stage healthcare technology startups.

Prior to his corporate and venture leadership, Adrian was an Assistant Professor of Mathematics at the University of Montevallo. He remains deeply committed to academic engagement and student mentorship, regularly giving guest lectures and keynotes to university students on AI, Data Science, and Startup Strategy. He recently delivered a talk at NC State University on navigating AI startup commercialization, and actively supports graduate data science programs at Washington University in St. Louis.

Adrian holds 1 issued U.S. patent (*U.S. Patent 2021009200*) with 4 additional U.S. patents pending. He has delivered keynotes to audiences of up to 5,000 attendees on Digital Fluency, Machine Learning, Operations Research, and Supply Chain Transformation, and is a published author of peer-reviewed research papers in pure and applied mathematics.

He serves on the Alumni Board of the University of Mississippi College of Liberal Arts and is the Founder and Principal of Cartier Holdings, LLC. Adrian earned his PhD, M.S., and B.A. in Mathematics from the University of Mississippi.

In his free time, Adrian enjoys spending time with his wife Dana and daughter Alexandria. He can occasionally be found golfing, playing soccer, bourbon tasting, or cheering on his alma mater, the Ole Miss Rebels, every fall.
